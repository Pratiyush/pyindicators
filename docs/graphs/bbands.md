# `bbands` — visualization ideas

> **Bollinger Bands, BBANDS** · category **volatility** · reads `close` · outputs `bb_middle`, `bb_upper`, `bb_lower`, `bb_bandwidth`, `bb_pctb`

**What it measures.** an SMA envelope at +/- ``mult`` population stdevs, with %B and bandwidth.

## Where to draw it

**Overlay on the price chart as a channel/band** — draw the upper and lower lines and **shade the area between them**; plot the middle line dashed. Price riding the bands is the signal.

## Scale & reference lines

Same **price scale** as the candles (no separate axis). 

## Rendering each output

- `bb_middle` → the **centre line**, drawn dashed.
- `bb_upper` → a **band edge**; shade between upper and lower.
- `bb_lower` → a **band edge**; shade between upper and lower.
- `bb_bandwidth` → a **band edge**; shade between upper and lower.
- `bb_pctb` → a line.

## Visual signals to highlight

- **Band touches / breaks** — closes outside a band flag stretch or breakout.
- **Squeeze** — bands narrowing = low volatility, often before a move; shade the squeeze.
- **Walking the band** — successive closes hugging one band = strong trend.
